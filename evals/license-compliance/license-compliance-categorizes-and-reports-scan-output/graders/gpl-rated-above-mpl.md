PASS if GPL-3.0 is rated high risk and described as potentially forcing the whole project onto the same license, while MPL-2.0 is rated a lower, medium risk as weak copyleft.

FAIL if both are rated at the same level, if a risk rating is omitted, or if the ratings are given without saying that GPL-3.0 can force the whole project onto the same license and that MPL-2.0 is weak copyleft.
